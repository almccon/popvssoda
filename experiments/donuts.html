Pop vs Soda donuts

Each state, province and territory is a donut sized by its number of responses, or by its population in 2010 (the US census, and Statistics Canada's estimates), split into the share answering pop, soda, coke or something else. Donuts are nudged apart so they don't overlap, so each sits near (not exactly on) the center of its state or province's responses. Each view is scaled to its own biggest donut, so check the size key when switching. Built from data/state_counts.csv.

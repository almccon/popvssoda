Pop vs Soda choropleth

The main county map extended into Canada. Canada's closest equivalent to a US county is the census division: counties in Ontario and the Maritimes, regional county municipalities in Quebec, regional districts in British Columbia, and numbered divisions on the Prairies. Colors mix the shares of responses like the main map: yellow for pop, cyan for soda, magenta for coke, and darker for "other". Canadian responses only give a three-character postal prefix, so each prefix is counted in the division that holds most of its land. Canada has fewer responses per unit than the US, so raise the minimum to hide noisy places. Built from data/county_counts.csv and data/census_division_counts.csv.

Pop vs Soda experiments

Work-in-progress ways of mapping the survey results. They're rough, and may change or disappear.

  • Counties and census divisions

    The main county map extended into Canada, with census divisions (Canada's closest equivalent to counties) in the same CMY mix. Switch between US and Canada, US only and Canada only, and hide places with too few responses.

    US counties and Canadian census divisions

  • Isoglosses

    Smoothed contour lines around areas where pop, soda, coke or "other" reaches 50%, 75% or 90% of nearby responses ("other" also gets 10%, 25% and 33% lines). You can switch between a fixed-radius, Gaussian or adaptive window and change its size.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • ZIP Voronoi

    One cell per ZIP code or FSA, colored with the main map's CMY mix. Cells stop at the country (or state and province) border, and don't reach further into empty land than they do anywhere in the lower 48.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • ZIP circles

    One circle per ZIP code or FSA, sized by its number of responses and colored with the main map's CMY mix. No layout: the circles overlap where they fall, biggest at the back.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • Hex bubbles

    Like county bubbles, but the ZIP codes and FSAs are first gathered into hexagons of equal size, so the starting areas are regular. You can change the hexagon size, and outline each state and province with a border that swells to hold its own bubbles.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • County bubbles

    Up to four circles per county, one for each answer, or one circle per county in the main map's CMY mix, sized by the number of responses or by county population and pushed apart so they don't overlap. They bubble up from south to north as the layout settles. State outlines can swell along with them.

    US counties

  • State donuts

    One donut per state, province and territory, sized by the number of responses and split by answer. Show the US and Canada together or either one alone.

    US states and Canadian provinces

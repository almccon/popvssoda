Pop vs Soda experiments

Work-in-progress ways of mapping the survey results. They're rough, and may change or disappear.

  • Isoglosses

    Smoothed contour lines around areas where pop, soda, coke or "other" reaches 25%, 33%, 50%, 75% or 90% of nearby responses. You can switch between a fixed-radius, Gaussian or adaptive window and change its size.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • ZIP Voronoi

    One cell per ZIP code or FSA, colored with the main map's CMY mix. Cells stop at the country (or state and province) border, and don't reach further into empty land than they do anywhere in the lower 48.

    ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs

  • County circles

    Up to four circles per county, one for each answer, sized by the number of responses and pushed apart so they don't overlap.

    US counties

  • State donuts

    One donut per state, sized by the number of responses and split by answer.

    US states

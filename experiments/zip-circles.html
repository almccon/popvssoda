Pop vs Soda ZIP circles

One circle for each US ZIP code and Canadian forward sortation area (FSA), with area proportional to its number of responses. Colors mix the shares of responses like the main map's CMY scheme: yellow for pop, cyan for soda, magenta for coke, and darker for "other". Circles sit at their ZIP code's point and overlap freely; the biggest are drawn first, so small places stay on top. Built from data/postal_counts.csv.

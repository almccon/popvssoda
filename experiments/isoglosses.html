Pop vs Soda isoglosses

At every spot on the map, add up the responses from nearby ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs, and work out the share for each term. "Nearby" can be a fixed radius, a Gaussian weighting that fades with distance, or an adaptive window that grows until it holds a set number of responses. Filled areas with lines show where pop, soda or coke is the answer of more than 50%, 75% and 90% of nearby respondents. "Other" rarely reaches a majority, so it also gets gray lines at 10%, 25% and 33%, over a dot pattern that gets denser at each level; where it does pass 50% it's filled gray like the others. Use the key to switch terms and levels on and off. Spots with too few responses nearby are left blank. Built from data/postal_counts.csv.

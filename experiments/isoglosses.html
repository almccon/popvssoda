Pop vs Soda isoglosses

At every spot on the map, add up the responses from nearby ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs, and work out the share for each term. "Nearby" can be a fixed radius, a Gaussian weighting that fades with distance, or an adaptive window that grows until it holds a set number of responses. Filled areas with solid lines show where pop, soda, coke or "other" is the answer of more than 50%, 75% and 90% of nearby respondents; dashed lines mark where a term reaches a quarter or a third, even if it isn't the majority. Use the key to switch terms and levels on and off. Spots with too few responses nearby are left blank. Built from data/postal_counts.csv.

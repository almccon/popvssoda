Pop vs Soda hex bubbles

The US ZIP codes and Canadian FSAs are first gathered into hexagons of equal size. Each hexagon then gets up to four circles: pop, soda, coke and other, each with area proportional to the number of people there who gave that answer. The circles start at the hexagon's center and push each other apart, bubbling up from the south as they grow. With outlines on, hexagons are split at state and province lines, and each state starts as its border, which swells and shifts with its bubbles. Built from data/postal_counts.csv.

ABOUT

The Pop vs Soda Page is a web-based project to plot the regional variations in the use of the terms "pop" and "soda" (and "coke") to describe carbonated soft drinks.

Respondents were asked for the generic word they use, and for the zip code of their hometown: the place where they learned the dialect of English they speak.

READING THE MAP

In the full-color views, each county (or, in Canada, census division, its closest equivalent) is colored by the mix of answers given by people from there, as shown in the triangle legend.

  • Yellow means pop
  • Cyan means soda
  • Magenta means coke

Counties where people split between terms get blended colors. The more people who answered something else ("other"), the darker the county. White counties have no responses.

Choose a single term to see what share of each county uses it. Hover over (or tap) a county to see its numbers.

Some counties have only a handful of responses, so a single person can give a county a strong color. Treat isolated bright counties with caution.
